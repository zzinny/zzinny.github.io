Empty

2022, experimental film, black&white, 7' 31''

Logline

Having long been lost, A drifts through the city until finally meeting B.

Note

The material abundance of the city, sustained at the cost of relentless and rapid change, paradoxically leaves me, as one of its inhabitants, with the feeling that something has been lost. The problem is that I do not know what that lost thing is. The film begins from this loss of orientation. It does not know when A, wandering aimlessly after losing their way, will arrive at a destination, nor does it know what that destination might be. A soon encounters B, and then parts ways with them. In the meeting of these anonymous protagonists, there is both an encounter and a farewell, yet there are no proper names by which to remember one another. Deprived of such names, their encounter, too, dissolves as something temporary and fleeting.
